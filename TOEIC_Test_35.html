TOEIC Test 35

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences — 30 questions

Directions : A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given. Select the best answer to complete the sentence, then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D). You have 15 minutes.
Question 101
When the committee members met last week, _______ approved plans to build a new library.
A subject pronoun is needed to replace the committee members and be the subject of approved. (D) they is correct. (B) is possessive; (C) is object; (A) is reflexive.
Question 102
Any questions about payroll should be sent _______ Ms. Pryor in the accounting department.
The phrasal verb is send to (a person). (B) to is the correct preposition indicating the recipient. (A), (C), and (D) do not fit this context.
Question 103
_______ the Lado factory and the Orden factory lost power last night.
(C) Both refers to two specific items together. (A) Either implies one or the other; (D) Each emphasizes individuality; (B) Several implies more than two.
Question 104
Job fair participants will not be allowed into the venue without _______ attire.
An adjective is needed to modify the noun attire. (A) suitable is the correct adjective. (B) and (D) are nouns; (C) is an adverb.
Question 105
Receipts for business purchases must be submitted _______ to the accounting department for reimbursement.
(D) promptly = without delay, fits the formal business context of a submission deadline. (A), (B), (C) do not make logical sense in this context.
Question 106
Please contact us with _______ questions regarding the warranty for your new backpack.
(A) any = one or more (open invitation). The sentence invites all possible questions. (B) every requires singular noun; (D) is negative; (C) does not fit.
Question 107
Reld Bank has a branch located _______ the Cheswin Mall.
(C) inside = within the physical space of the mall. (A) about means approximately; (B) into implies movement; (D) between requires two reference points.
Question 108
Zinny Sunscreen offers _______ twice as much protection from the Sun's harmful rays as other leading brands.
An adverb is needed to modify the quantity expression twice as much. (A) nearly = almost, is the correct adverb. (B) is a noun; (C) and (D) are adjective forms.
Question 109
Decorations selected for the restaurant should be _______ with the building's historic nature.
(C) compatible with = harmoniously matching. This is the standard adjective + preposition collocation for describing elements that fit together well.
Question 110
Hotel guests needing _______ with the television should contact the front desk by calling extension four.
A noun is needed after the verb needing. (B) assistance is the correct noun form meaning help. (A) is a person; (C) and (D) are verb forms.
Question 111
Our continued success in the electronics market is highly _______ on the level of research investments.
After is highly, an adjective is needed. (C) dependent forms the structure is dependent on = relies on. (A) and (B) are verb forms; (D) is a noun.
Question 112
After much discussion, Murphy's Home Products _______ agreed to extend the deadline for rebate submissions.
(C) finally = after a long time or deliberation, fits the context of after much discussion. (A) means repeatedly; (B) and (D) do not fit semantically.
Question 113
Because _______ assembly of the machine is vital, please follow the instructions included with the materials.
An adjective is needed before the noun assembly. (A) accurate correctly modifies the noun. (B) is an adverb; (C) and (D) are nouns.
Question 114
By the end of next year, Line Zone Sports _______ its tenth superstore in the area.
The time marker by the end of next year requires the future perfect. (D) will have opened expresses an action completed before a future point.
Question 115
Drivers should expect twenty-minute _______ on Route 35 between Front and Maple Streets throughout the summer.
In a traffic context, delays are what drivers expect. (C) delays = periods of waiting. Twenty-minute delays is a standard traffic term.
Question 116
Mr. Akiyama is looking for a moving company that can pack his fragile art pieces _______.
An adverb is needed to modify the verb pack. (B) carefully is the correct adverb form. (A) is a noun/verb; (C) is a past tense verb; (D) is an adjective.
Question 117
The renovation of Shireen's Sandwich Shop was completed _______ the two-month time frame.
(A) within + time period = before the end of that period. Completed within the two-month time frame means it was finished before the deadline.
Question 118
Mr. Morales had to travel on a different day _______ the flight he had planned to take was fully booked.
(A) because introduces the cause or reason. The sentence explains why Mr. Morales changed his travel day. (D) despite would require a noun/gerund phrase, not a clause.
Question 119
Although Liam O'Mally's new novel is a fictionalized account of early space travel, it draws on _______ events.
The novel is fictional but based on real past events. (B) historical = relating to history, is the correct adjective. The other options are semantically incompatible with events in this context.
Question 120
At Temistow Corporation, _______ who work at least 30 hours a week qualify for full-time status.
(D) those is used as a pronoun referring to a general group of people: those who work… This is a standard structure. (A), (B), (C) refer to things nearby, not a general group.
Question 121
Bakerton Park's tree identification course is designed to _______ your knowledge of local forest life.
(B) expand = to make greater in scope. Expand your knowledge is a standard collocation. (A) and (D) do not collocate with knowledge; (C) means to obtain.
Question 122
Department managers would like _______ everyone to use the Market Street entrance on Monday.
The structure would like + to-infinitive: would like to remind everyone. (B) to remind is the correct infinitive form after would like.
Question 123
Please note that _______ media reports, Friday's orchestra performance at the Morlin Civic Center has not been canceled.
(C) contrary to + noun phrase = in opposition to. Contrary to media reports means the media said it was canceled but it was not. (D) would mean the reports support the claim.
Question 124
The receipt of the grant was a big step _______ getting the bridge project completed.
(D) toward = in the direction of a goal. A step toward getting X done is a standard English expression. (B) away would mean moving away from the goal.
Question 125
Gevrin Technologies' customer service chat window helps ensure that customers on their Web site receive timely _______.
A noun is needed as the object of receive. (D) responses = answers/replies, is the correct noun. (A) and (C) are verb forms; (B) is an adjective.
Question 126
The project Mr. Brooks was assigned is expected to take an _______ amount of time to complete.
(A) immense = very large. In context, the project will take a very long time. An immense amount of time is the natural collocation. (B), (C), and (D) do not fit semantically.
Question 127
The corporate _______ of Darton Wares, Inc., is complex, but the chart illustrates how its departments are organized.
(B) structure = the way something is organized. Corporate structure is a standard business term referring to the hierarchy and organization of a company.
Question 128
Sumi Koyama's book is _______ a collection of remembrances from her fascinating career.
An adverb is needed to modify the entire predicate is a collection of. (D) essentially = basically, in essence. (A) and (B) are nouns; (C) is a verb.
Question 129
Highway construction _______ scheduled to start this June has been delayed, according to the mayor's office.
(D) initially = originally, at first. The construction was originally planned for June but has been delayed. (A) exactly and (C) merely do not convey this original-plan meaning.
Question 130
Fabramet Ltd. is _______ information about its new clothing line until the official release date in July.
(C) withholding = deliberately not releasing. The company is keeping the information secret until July. Withhold information is the correct collocation here.
