TOEIC Test 34

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences — 30 questions

Directions : A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given. Select the best answer to complete the sentence, then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D). You have 15 minutes.
Question 101
Doctors at Amcair Medical take the time to listen _______ to patients.
An adverb is needed to modify the verb listen. (C) carefully is the correct adverb form. (A) and (D) are wrong forms; (B) care is a noun/verb.
Question 102
Ms. Watanabe is expected _______ to the office by 2:00 PM today.
Expect takes a to-infinitive complement: is expected to return. Only (D) provides the infinitive form.
Question 103
Please be sure to check in promptly _______ your shift starts and be ready to assist customers.
A temporal subordinating conjunction is needed. (D) when introduces the time clause correctly. (A), (B), and (C) do not fit grammatically here.
Question 104
Many activities take place at the library, both official events and _______ gatherings.
An adjective is needed before the noun gatherings. (A) informal is the correct adjective. (B) is an adverb, (D) is a noun.
Question 105
Distal Resources has closed its zinc mine because the operational costs are now _______ high.
The structure too + adjective expresses excess. (D) too = excessively. (B) enough comes after the adjective (high enough), not before it.
Question 106
Temporary parking passes will be issued to all conference _______.
A noun is needed after the preposition to as the object. (D) participants (people who participate) is the correct noun form.
Question 107
Refer to the school's Web site for information regarding _______ admissions policy.
A possessive adjective is needed before the noun policy. (B) our modifies the noun directly. (C) ours is a standalone pronoun; (A) and (D) are object/reflexive forms.
Question 108
Mr. Hibiki has added plumbing supplies to his wholesale line of maintenance _______.
In context, a general product category noun is needed. (C) items fits naturally: maintenance items. The other nouns do not collocate appropriately here.
Question 109
To _______ discounted rates, sign up for our rewards plan.
The verb must collocate with discounted rates. (A) receive is the standard collocation: to receive a discount/rate. The other verbs do not fit semantically.
Question 110
Ms. Lopez will submit a _______ of the important points discussed at the budget meeting.
A noun meaning a condensed account is needed. (B) summary fits perfectly: a summary of the important points. (A), (C), and (D) are contextually wrong.
Question 111
The manufacturer promises that the replacement desks for our office will be much _______.
After much in a comparative context, a comparative adjective is used. (B) sturdier = more sturdy. (A) is the base adjective; (C) is a noun; (D) is an adverb.
Question 112
The Sunnyville farmers market should be set up and open _______ two weeks.
(D) within + time period expresses a deadline: open within two weeks. (C) through would need a definite end point; (A) and (B) do not collocate here.
Question 113
Dreamways Corporation has forecasted a profit _______ of 3 percent for the coming year.
A noun is needed after profit to form a compound noun. (A) growth gives profit growth — a standard business term. (B), (C), (D) are verb forms.
Question 114
The last evening of the Pineville Air show, featuring stunt and formation fliers, will _______ with a fireworks display.
After the auxiliary will, the bare infinitive is required. (A) conclude is correct. (B) is a participle; (C) is third-person present; (D) is past tense.
Question 115
Mechanics must complete a detailed log of service repairs for _______ by 5:00 PM each workday.
After the preposition for, a noun is required. (C) approval is the correct noun form: for approval.
Question 116
The Cajulus Company is inviting construction firms to _______ proposals for the new building project.
The standard collocation in business English is to submit a proposal. (C) submit is the only verb that fits logically here.
Question 117
Wire NM, the television channel known for covering business news, is now valued _______ roughly $1 billion.
The fixed expression is valued at + amount. (B) at is the required preposition in this financial context.
Question 118
The Sea View Apartments are _______ renovated and ready for occupancy.
An adverb is needed to modify the past participle renovated. (B) freshly = recently. (A) is a superlative adjective; (C) is comparative; (D) is a verb.
Question 119
_______ weather conditions resulted in the postponement of the outdoor concert scheduled for today.
Context implies the weather was not anticipated. (B) Unexpected = not foreseen, fits logically. (A) means conclusive; (C) means resolute; (D) means demanding.
Question 120
When picking up tickets from the box office, patrons must show _______ a hard copy or an electronic copy of their receipt.
The paired conjunction is either… or. (C) either introduces the first of two alternatives. (A) is for yes/no questions; (B) and (D) alone do not form the required structure.
Question 121
We apologize for last week's shipping delay, which was _______ caused by a backorder from our supplier.
(D) primarily = mainly, chiefly — the most natural adverb to explain the main cause. (A) rarely contradicts the apologetic tone; (B) and (C) do not fit the context.
Question 122
The robust sales of Fujon phones can be attributed _______ to in-store promotions than to expanding markets.
The structure more… than is used for comparisons. (A) more completes the comparative construction: attributed more to X than to Y.
Question 123
For any Charrington kitchen appliance, a fee of $79.95 _______ the one-year service plan to three years.
An adjective is needed to describe fee (a fee that can extend the plan). (B) extendable = capable of being extended. Note: a fee extendable functions as a post-modifier here.
Question 124
The tour will stop at the former home of artist Colette Benoit, _______ paintings hang in museums across Europe.
A possessive relative pronoun is needed to show that the paintings belong to Colette Benoit. (C) whose is the only possessive relative pronoun available.
Question 125
Once construction of the Western Coastal Parkway is complete, motorists will be able to visit places that had been _______.
The sentence implies places that could not be reached before. (A) inaccessible = not accessible, fits perfectly. The other adjectives are semantically incorrect for places.
Question 126
Chandon Clothing employees must not hold items for customers _______ they have received prior permission from the manager.
(B) unless introduces a conditional exception: must not do X unless Y. This is the standard structure for negative conditions. (A), (C), (D) change the meaning illogically.
Question 127
Lehmann Semiconductors _______ announced that its product development team has been awarded the annual Hahn Award for Innovation.
(D) proudly conveys that the announcement is made with pride, which fits the positive news context. (A) and (C) modify adjectives/adverbs; (B) implies uncertainty, which contradicts the factual announcement.
Question 128
The first invoice is for the equipment purchase, and _______ is for installation.
With exactly two items, English uses the one… the other. (B) the other refers to the second of two invoices. (A) the others implies more than two.
Question 129
The information technology director has asked for _______ on the new software being used in the sales department.
(C) feedback = opinions/reactions about something, the standard business term for input on a new system. Ask for feedback is a fixed collocation.
Question 130
For specific washing instructions, please refer to the product care _______ sewn to each pillow.
A care label is the standard term for the tag sewn into a textile product giving washing instructions. (A) label is the only word that collocates correctly here.
