TOEIC Test 38

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences — 30 questions

Directions : A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given. Select the best answer to complete the sentence, then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D). You have 15 minutes.
Question 101
The wellness center offers _______ massages to its staff members each month.
An adjective is needed to modify the noun massages. (D) free = at no cost. (A) and (B) are verb forms; (C) is an adverb and would modify a verb, not a noun.
Question 102
Because of the higher _______ of metals, Hydrotep's annual purchasing budget must be increased.
(D) price = the cost of metals. When the price of raw materials rises, purchasing budgets must increase. Higher price of metals is the natural collocation in a financial context.
Question 103
Mr. Yamamoto and I will arrive on time as long as _______ train is not delayed.
A possessive adjective is needed before the noun train. (A) our correctly modifies train (belonging to both Mr. Yamamoto and the speaker). (B) and (C) are pronouns; (D) is reflexive.
Question 104
Anyone willing to _______ books to the Rosen School's annual book sale should contact Bill Choi.
(A) donate = to give something as a gift or for a charitable purpose. Donate books to a sale is the standard collocation. (B) means to go to; (C) and (D) are unrelated verbs.
Question 105
Tourists are encouraged to travel to Sri Lanka in November, when skies are _______ and temperatures are comfortable.
After the linking verb are, an adjective is needed to describe the skies. (A) clear = free from clouds. (B) is an adverb; (C) is a noun; (D) is a verb.
Question 106
Telepress Cable Service now permits customers to pay _______ for the channels they select.
(D) only = exclusively, solely. Pay only for the channels they select means they are charged for nothing beyond what they choose. (A), (B), and (C) do not fit this meaning.
Question 107
Last year the Kreger Corporation _______ for the Best Workplace in the City Award.
Last year requires the simple past. The corporation did not nominate itself; it was nominated (passive voice). (D) was nominated = passive past. (A) is present; (B) active past; (C) present continuous.
Question 108
The Kemmen Industrial Group has _______ installed a new wastewater recovery system.
(C) successfully = in a way that achieves the goal. Has successfully installed indicates the installation was completed effectively. (A), (B), (D) do not collocate meaningfully with installed here.
Question 109
The artists and editors will vote today for the magazine cover _______ prefer.
A subject pronoun is needed to be the subject of the relative clause verb prefer. (D) they refers to the artists and editors. (A) is possessive; (B) reflexive; (C) object.
Question 110
Passengers may choose _______ vegetarian and nonvegetarian meals on all international flights.
(B) between is used when choosing from two specific options. Choose between vegetarian and nonvegetarian presents exactly two alternatives. (A), (C), (D) are directional prepositions that do not fit.
Question 111
Parking for Whelan Inn employees _______ in the garage adjacent to the hotel.
The subject Parking is singular and uncountable, requiring a singular verb. (A) is located is the correct passive present form. (C) uses plural verb; (B) and (D) are incomplete forms.
Question 112
The Doubly Great package offered by Lemonwood Spa includes a light meal with _______ a massage or a facial.
The paired conjunction either… or presents one of two alternatives. (D) either introduces the first option (massage or facial). (A) both…and would mean both are included; the sentence implies a choice.
Question 113
The human resources director asked _______ to approve the training schedule for next quarter.
An object pronoun is needed as the object of asked. (C) me is correct: asked me to approve. (A) is reflexive; (B) and (D) are possessive forms.
Question 114
Client records must be returned to the file room _______ the end of each business day.
(C) at is used with specific points in time: at the end of the day. This is a fixed time expression. (A) in is used for periods; (B) on for dates/days; (D) does not fit.
Question 115
The shipment of tires was delayed but will arrive _______ two days.
(B) within + time period = before the end of that period. Arrive within two days = arrive before two days have passed. (A) means in fact; (C) means separated; (D) means in the end.
Question 116
Although Pennly Restaurant is _______ closed on Sundays, it makes exceptions for special events.
(C) normally = usually, as a rule. The word Although signals a contrast: the restaurant is normally closed but makes exceptions. (A) does not fit; (B) means every day; (D) expresses uncertainty.
Question 117
Mr. Bates announced today that Bates Transportation _______ with Rolfson Trucking at the end of this month.
The event is announced as happening at the end of this month = in the future. (D) will be merging = future continuous, indicating an action in progress at a future time. (C) implies it already happened.
Question 118
Tecury Mills, a company which sells most of its textiles in India, is _______ to widen its customer base.
(B) seeking = trying to find/achieve. Seeking to widen its customer base = trying to expand its clients. Seek + to-infinitive is a standard collocation.
Question 119
The Wilson Canning Machine ensures that jars are _______ sealed before they are packed for delivery.
An adverb is needed to modify the adjective/past participle sealed. (B) securely = in a secure manner. (A) and (D) are adjective/verb forms; (C) is a third-person verb.
Question 120
Due to the rain, the reception will be held in Ballroom A instead _______ in the Founders' Garden.
(B) instead of is the fixed prepositional phrase meaning in place of. Instead of in the Founders' Garden = rather than in the garden. This is a fixed two-word preposition.
Question 121
Mr. Ostweiler was commended for his _______ in the product rollout.
After the preposition for his, a noun is required. (A) involvement = the fact of being involved, is the correct noun form. (B) and (C) are verb forms; (D) is a past participle.
Question 122
Among the machinists at the Newville factory, Peter Sakano is one of the _______.
One of the + superlative adjective + plural noun is the standard structure. (D) most industrious = the superlative of industrious (hardworking). (A) and (C) are nouns; (B) is a non-superlative adjective.
Question 123
Company finance expert Emily Lareau has _______ the operating budget numerous times in response to executive feedback.
(B) amended = revised or modified (a document, plan, or budget). Amend the budget is the standard term for official revisions. (A) means predicted; (C) means formally stated; (D) means fulfilled.
Question 124
All male employees must wear button-down, collared shirts, but ties are _______.
(C) optional = not required, left to individual choice. The contrast with must wear (mandatory) makes optional the logical choice for ties. (A), (B), and (D) do not fit this context.
Question 125
_______ the design team was very busy this week, the report will not be ready on time.
(B) Since introduces a causal clause with a full subject + verb. Since [subject] [verb] = because. (C) and (D) require noun phrases, not full clauses. (A) introduces a time clause which doesn't fit the causal meaning.
Question 126
The success of last week's fund-raising event was a result of the _______ between Kelmon's marketing and development divisions.
After the preposition of the, a noun is required. (A) collaboration = cooperative work. Collaboration between departments is the standard business expression. (B) is a verb; (C) is a person; (D) is an adverb.
Question 127
The receipt of the grant was a big step _______ getting the bridge project completed.
(D) toward = in the direction of a goal. A step toward getting X done is a standard English expression indicating progress. (B) away would mean moving further from the goal.
Question 128
Stieg Art Gallery will be holding an interactive _______ in July on the history of sculpture.
(D) exhibit = a public display or show in a gallery. An interactive exhibit on the history of sculpture is natural museum/gallery language. (A), (B), and (C) do not fit this context.
Question 129
Varten Motors has designed a new engine for cargo ships that works _______ than the previous models.
The word than signals a comparative. An adverb is needed to modify the verb works. (A) more efficiently is the correct comparative adverb form. (B) is a superlative adjective; (C) and (D) lack the comparative.
Question 130
Despite his lack of formal training, Mr. Tung is a highly _______ negotiator.
(A) effective = producing the desired result, successful. A highly effective negotiator is a natural collocation. (B) means describing; (C) relates to celebrations; (D) means uncertain/hesitant.
