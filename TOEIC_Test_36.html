TOEIC Test 36

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences — 30 questions

Directions : A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given. Select the best answer to complete the sentence, then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D). You have 15 minutes.
Question 101
Employees will be told several more _______ about evening work shifts.
A plural noun is needed after more and before the prepositional phrase. (C) details = specific pieces of information. (B) detail is singular; (A) and (D) are verb forms.
Question 102
All maintenance questions should be directed _______ Rex Burr at extension 151.
The phrasal verb is direct something to someone. (D) to is the correct preposition indicating the recipient.
Question 103
Grimaud Holdings is extremely _______ to be named the top producer of adhesives.
After the linking verb is + adverb extremely, an adjective is needed. (B) proud is correct. (A) is comparative; (C) is an adverb; (D) is a noun.
Question 104
Mr. Hennin would like the table linens _______ to his restaurant by Saturday.
(B) delivered fits the context: you deliver physical items (linens) to a location. Would like [them] delivered is the correct causative structure.
Question 105
Last month the department spent €532, _______ taxes and fees, on training materials for lab technicians.
(D) including = comprising as part of the total. €532, including taxes and fees is the standard structure to specify that taxes are part of the amount.
Question 106
Jackson Road has seen _______ severe traffic jams following the opening of Groff Arena.
An adverb is needed to modify the adjective severe. (C) increasingly = more and more. (A) and (D) are verb forms; (B) is an infinitive.
Question 107
At least two-thirds of shareholders must agree on the proposal, _______ it will not pass.
(C) or introduces the alternative/consequence: agree, or it will not pass. This is a conditional structure using or. (B) however shows contrast, not condition.
Question 108
Refunds from Silverton Wireless will be issued _______ two weeks of a service cancellation.
(A) within + time = before the end of that period. Issued within two weeks sets a deadline. (B) until would need a specific date/event as its endpoint.
Question 109
While Professor Bitok is on sabbatical, Dr. Neil Lin will take on some of _______ teaching responsibilities.
A possessive adjective is needed before the noun responsibilities. (D) her correctly modifies the noun. (A) hers is a standalone pronoun; (B) reflexive; (C) subject pronoun.
Question 110
Our accounting software is currently being tested, but we expect it to be fully _______ by next week.
(C) operational = functioning, in working order. Software becomes fully operational when it is ready to use. The other adjectives do not collocate with software in this context.
Question 111
_______ over a company's Web site can help job seekers gain useful insights about a prospective employer.
A gerund is needed as the subject of the sentence. (D) Reading (over a Web site) functions as a noun subject. (A) is the base verb; (B) is third-person singular; (C) is a person.
Question 112
The study will be released _______ our experimental results are confirmed.
(B) once = as soon as / when. Released once results are confirmed = released as soon as the results are confirmed. (A) introduces a yes/no alternative; (C) and (D) do not fit.
Question 113
With its two new plants nearing _______, Radkov Auto is now focusing on maximizing its production capacity.
After the verb nearing, a noun is needed as its object. (C) completion = the act of completing. Nearing completion is a fixed expression meaning almost finished.
Question 114
The changes to the original phone design _______ an improvement in quality and convenience.
The subject The changes is plural, requiring a plural verb. (A) represent is correct. (B) is third-person singular; (C) is a participle; (D) is an adjective/noun.
Question 115
Han Sun-mi has _______ led Harmun Motors for more than twenty years.
An adverb is needed to modify the verb led in the present perfect. (D) successfully is the correct adverb form. (A) and (C) are nouns; (B) is an adjective.
Question 116
_______ in the Penmuse Association is open to professional journalists and publishers.
(C) Membership in an organization is open to qualified individuals. Membership in the Penmuse Association is the natural subject for this sentence about who can join.
Question 117
Karrow Group's dairy business was not very _______ until they adopted modern farming methods.
After was not very (linking verb + adverb), an adjective is needed. (A) profitable = generating profit. (B) and (C) are verb forms; (D) is an adverb.
Question 118
Receiving a 10 percent discount on all store purchases is a job _______ offered to every ZNV Hardware employee.
(B) benefit = an advantage or perk offered with a job. A discount is a classic example of a job benefit. (A), (C), and (D) do not collocate with this context.
Question 119
The information published in The Southwood Business Journal is _______ quite accurate.
(C) generally = usually, most of the time. This qualifier softens the absolute claim: the journal is generally accurate, not always. (A), (B), (D) do not fit logically.
Question 120
Harner Advertising submitted a _______ offer but did not win the contract.
An adjective is needed before the noun offer. (B) competitive = strong enough to compete with others. (A) and (C) are verb/adverb forms; (D) is a noun.
Question 121
Because of funding limitations, Mr. Shang _______ the proposal to replace the air-conditioning system.
(B) rejected = refused to accept. Due to limited funding, the proposal was turned down. Reject a proposal is the standard collocation in a budget context.
Question 122
Though their sales presentation was brief, Kwon & Kessler made the best case for hiring them _______ our accountants.
(D) as introduces a role or capacity: hire them as accountants. This is the correct preposition for describing in what capacity someone is hired.
Question 123
Staff writers should not publish _______ online without the approval of the editorial committee.
In a negative sentence (should not), use anything (not nothing). (D) anything is correct: should not publish anything. (A) and (C) create a double negative.
Question 124
Expanding the number of staff on the product development team would lead to greater _______.
(D) innovation = new ideas and creativity. More staff on a product team leads to greater innovation. (A), (B), and (C) are contextually inappropriate.
Question 125
Mr. Sanz's raise is retroactive, so he will be given a credit to boost the salary amount he was _______ paid.
(B) previously = before now, in the past. Since the raise is retroactive, it applies to amounts previously paid. (A), (C), and (D) do not make logical sense here.
Question 126
The cost of repairing the old printer _______ the cost of replacing it.
The conditional context (implied: if we repaired it) requires the conditional form. (D) would exceed is the correct conditional. Note: (A) needs -s for third person; (C) reverses the meaning.
Question 127
Drivers for Calner Hotel's airport pickup service must be at the terminal ten minutes before the guest's _______ arrival time.
(A) anticipated = expected. Anticipated arrival time = the scheduled/expected time of arrival. This is a standard collocation in hospitality and transport.
Question 128
The monthly amount billed by Newpro Cellular is fixed for the _______ of the contract.
(D) duration = the entire length of time. For the duration of the contract is a fixed legal/business expression meaning throughout the contract period.
Question 129
Despite experiencing a _______ decline in business over the summer, most restaurants rebounded when the weather cooled.
An adjective is needed before the noun decline. (A) moderate = not extreme. (B) is a person; (C) is an adverb; (D) is a noun.
Question 130
If adequate funds are _______ to Gosse Medical Center, it will pursue nutrition research.
(B) allocated = officially assigned/distributed. Funds are allocated to an organization is the standard financial collocation. (A), (C), (D) do not collocate with funds in this way.
