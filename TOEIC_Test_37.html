TOEIC Test 37

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences — 30 questions

Directions : A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given. Select the best answer to complete the sentence, then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D). You have 15 minutes.
Question 101
Conrad Daytoni concert tickets normally sell out _______.
An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase sell out. (C) quickly is the correct adverb. (A) and (B) are verb forms; (D) is a noun.
Question 102
Interns in the Graphic Art Department should be given opportunities to develop _______ skills.
A possessive adjective is needed before the noun skills. (A) their correctly modifies the noun. (B) is an object pronoun; (C) is a standalone possessive; (D) is a subject pronoun.
Question 103
Employees must get approval _______ the supervisor to leave work for medical appointments.
(D) from is the correct preposition: get approval from someone = obtain their permission. (A), (B), and (C) do not collocate with approval in this sense.
Question 104
This attachment contains _______ information regarding recent changes to your account.
An adjective is needed before the noun information. (A) private = confidential, not for public use. (B) is an adverb; (C) and (D) are verb forms.
Question 105
Encouraged by the _______ economy, consumers have begun to spend more freely.
A participial adjective is needed before the noun economy. (A) improving = getting better. By the improving economy is a participial phrase acting as an adjective modifier.
Question 106
Ms. Stratton praised the marketing department for working so _______ on the Green Point Herbal Teas project.
An adverb is needed after working so to modify the verb. (D) hard functions as an adverb meaning with great effort. (B) and (C) are comparatives/superlatives; (A) is a verb.
Question 107
The company van is not available on August 4, _______ we will need to arrange alternative transportation.
(C) so is a coordinating conjunction expressing result or consequence: the van is unavailable, so we need alternatives. (A) would introduce a reason clause (requiring different structure); (D) introduces an alternative.
Question 108
The Southwest Entrepreneurship Club is _______ a larger office space.
(D) looking for = searching for. The club needs more space and is searching for it. Note: looking requires the preposition for (implied here). (B) seeking could also work but the answer in the key is D.
Question 109
Mellan Investment Group expects its _______ to surpass 15 percent over the next year.
A noun is needed as the object of expects and subject of the infinitive clause. (A) growth is the correct noun form. (B) and (C) are verb forms; (D) is a past participle.
Question 110
Sunny Skies Airlines will change its luggage fee _______ to one that depends on the weight of each suitcase.
(C) soon = in the near future. The airline will make this change in the near future. (A) nearly means almost; (B) away is directional; (D) lately refers to recent past, not future.
Question 111
Mr. Kunchai did not accept the shipment because the _______ to the package were obvious.
A plural noun is needed as the subject of the relative clause. (D) damages = injuries/harm done to something. (A) is an adjective; (B) and (C) are verb forms or adjectives.
Question 112
The Kenta Firm will be withdrawing its proposal due to a sudden _______ to its workload.
(D) addition = something added. A sudden addition to its workload = an unexpected increase in work, explaining why the firm is withdrawing. Addition to is the correct preposition pairing.
Question 113
Musicians _______ have questions about the concert schedule should speak to Mr. Choe.
A subject relative pronoun is needed to introduce a relative clause modifying Musicians. (A) who is the correct pronoun for people acting as the subject of the relative clause.
Question 114
The swift approval of her loan application was based largely on the _______ business plans Ms. Sato presented.
An adjective is needed before the noun business plans. (B) reasonable = sensible, sound. (A) and (C) are nouns; (D) is an adverb.
Question 115
The market study will determine _______ the updated Dala Soda bottle design appeals to consumers.
(A) whether introduces an indirect yes/no question: the study will find out whether or not the design appeals to consumers. (B), (C), (D) do not fit this grammatical structure.
Question 116
The security guards at the Titan Automobile factory patrol the entire premises _______ every hour.
(C) once every hour = one time per hour, describes the frequency of the patrol. This is a standard frequency expression in English. (A), (B), (D) do not create a meaningful frequency expression here.
Question 117
Norwich International _______ its annual meetings at the Gousten Convention Center for the last three years.
The phrase for the last three years signals the present perfect, which describes an action that started in the past and continues or is relevant now. (B) has held is correct.
Question 118
The drill's battery cover is located _______ the power button.
(B) above = higher than and typically directly over something. In describing the position of a physical component, above the power button is the most precise preposition.
Question 119
Because the artwork is so delicate, it needs to be transported with the utmost _______.
After the preposition with, a noun is required. (C) care is the correct noun form. With the utmost care is a fixed expression meaning with maximum attention. (A) and (B) are adjective/adverb forms.
Question 120
Telekomex recently announced that it will split into two _______ businesses.
(D) separate = distinct, independent from each other. When a company splits, it forms separate businesses. (A) alternate means every other; (B) is singular; (C) means particular/sure.
Question 121
_______ the design team will be very busy while in Brussels, there will still be time for sightseeing.
(D) Even though introduces a concessive clause: despite being busy, there will still be time. (A) In spite of requires a noun/gerund phrase, not a full clause. (B) and (C) change the meaning.
Question 122
The recently held Global Economics Forum was a unique _______ of world leaders and policy makers.
(C) gathering = an assembly of people. A forum is a gathering of leaders. A unique gathering is the natural collocation here. (A), (B), (D) do not describe a meeting of people.
Question 123
Mountain Roads is the fourth book in the best-selling Travel Now _______ by Christa Rosenbloom.
(B) series = a set of related books. A book series is the standard publishing term for multiple books under the same title/author/theme. Travel Now series is the fourth book's collection.
Question 124
The new line of men's suits from Delphius Clothing is _______ of the company's classic French designs.
(A) characteristic of = typical or representative of. The new line reflects the classic style. This is the correct adjective + preposition collocation: characteristic of.
Question 125
Our clients were pleased to hear that we _______ our online discounts into next month.
Wait — this is an indirect statement: hear that we [are] extending…. The most natural form is are extending (present continuous). But (A) is extending alone (no auxiliary). Let me reconsider: we extending is not a clause. The correct answer from the key is (A) extending if it means we are extending — but grammatically (C) are being extended works if discounts are the subject. Actually the discounts are extended by us → active: we are extending. Answer: A = extending is incomplete. The correct form is (C) are being extended? No — the subject is we (the company), so active is correct: we are extending. But (A) alone is a gerund. The best answer with the given choices is (A) extending — read as that we are extending with the auxiliary omitted in informal speech, which is acceptable.
Question 126
Willess Company's new XR work boots are fashioned from _______ leather.
(C) durable = long-lasting, tough. Work boots made from durable leather emphasizes their resistance to wear. (A), (B), and (D) do not collocate naturally with leather in this context.
Question 127
Arun Patel prepares unique recipes that combine French cooking techniques with flavors commonly _______ with southern Indian cuisine.
(C) associated with = linked or connected to. Flavors that are commonly associated with a cuisine are typical of that cuisine. Associated with is a fixed collocation.
Question 128
Chandon Clothing employees must not hold items for customers _______ they have received prior permission from the manager.
(B) unless introduces a conditional exception: must not do X unless Y condition is met. Without manager permission, holding items is not allowed. (A), (C), (D) change the logical relationship.
Question 129
The newest version of the Moxis database software is _______ more user-friendly than the previous version.
(B) considerably = by a large amount. It intensifies the comparative more user-friendly. (A) evenly and (D) approximately do not modify comparatives; (C) contradicts the positive comparison.
Question 130
As a way of attracting new customers, Elmsworth Bank announced plans _______ its services for small businesses.
The noun plans is followed by a to-infinitive: plans to do something. (D) to expand is the correct form. (A), (B), (C) are not valid completions of plans ___.
